## 24. Study Tips for A1–A2 Swedish

- Learn nouns together with their article: **en bok**, **ett hus**.
- Practice present tense verbs first: **jag är**, **jag har**, **jag vill**, **jag kan**.
- Learn common phrases as chunks, not only single words.
- Practice word order in simple sentences: **Jag bor i Sverige.**
- Use the vocabulary in short daily sentences.
- Review small groups of words regularly instead of memorizing long lists at once.
